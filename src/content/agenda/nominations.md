## What it is

The Committee on Nominations proposes the people who fill elected positions across
the conference's boards, commissions, and vision teams. The Discipline asks it to
work toward inclusiveness — clergywomen, youth and young adults, older adults, small
churches, people with disabilities, and racial and ethnic persons — and to balance
representation across districts.

## How it works at conference

The committee brings a **preliminary report** early, which includes the process for
**nominations from the floor**, and a **final report** acted on later in the session
(Rule 25). If you want to nominate someone, the preliminary report is the moment to
learn how.
