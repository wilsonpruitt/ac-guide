Annual Conference is composed of **clergy members** and an **equal number of lay members**
(Rule 15). Each charge elects a lay member, with additional lay members for additional
clergy, plus a long roster of officers and youth/young-adult representatives.

Because lay and clergy totals must balance, the conference assigns **additional lay
members** to districts by membership to equalize the two — and at least 10% of those
additional members must be age 30 or under. The conference also provides English and
Spanish editions of official materials and translation during sessions (Rule 18).
