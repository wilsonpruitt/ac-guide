To save floor time, two consent agendas bundle items expected to be uncontroversial
(Rule 23):

- **Consent Agenda 1** — information-only reports. The conference simply receives them.
- **Consent Agenda 2** — action items believed to have little or no dissent.

Any member may move to **remove** an item, with a second and a one-minute explanation. If
**20%** of members vote to remove it, it comes to the floor as a normal, debatable item.
Pulling something out is ordinary and expected — not an act of hostility.
